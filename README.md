# Kingdom
### The Kingdom anime plot centers on the ambition of a war orphan named Shin to become a "greatest general" in ancient China's Warring States Period, alongside his best friend Hyou, a slave. After Hyou is used as a body double in a palace coup, Shin encounters the young King Ei Sei, who resembles Hyou, and embarks on a quest to help the king reclaim his throne and ultimately unify China. The story follows their journey through a world of warfare, political intrigue, and strategic battles as Shin rises through the military ranks.
